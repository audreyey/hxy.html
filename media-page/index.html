Why Public Parks Matter

This page explains why public parks and urban green spaces are valuable. It combines an image, a video, a map, and short writing to show how city spaces support health, community, and the environment.

Nature in the middle of the city

Parks give people a place to rest, walk, exercise, and enjoy a break from busy streets. They also make neighborhoods feel more welcoming and balanced.

How green spaces help people

Green spaces can improve mental health, lower stress, support cleaner air, and give communities a shared place to gather.

Why this matters

Public parks are valuable because they give everyone access to safe outdoor space, even in crowded cities where private yards are rare.

They also support wildlife, reduce heat in dense neighborhoods, and create places where people can walk, read, talk, or simply breathe.
